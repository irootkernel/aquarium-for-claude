# Epic orchestration

The [adaptive execution contract](adaptive-execution.md) now selects adequate definitions and resolves current mapped placements for this family. The canonical node tables below retain their original meanings; adaptive dispatch uses actual role, capability, evidence and approved due ownership rather than node spelling. Original review, independent assessment, effect and acceptance boundaries remain due.

## Ordinary execution and Evaluator continuity

Goal version `37` uses ordinary Actions under the [v3 contract](ordinary-agent-orchestration.md).
Task phase skills do not govern goal-centered Epic work. Supply original criteria,
current candidate, evidence, checks, exact authority and available public handles.
Retain one logical native Evaluator across members, corrections, internal validation
and closeout when supported. Reconnect known handles or restore original criteria
and current evidence to a fresh independent Evaluator; private executor history and
configuration digests are not prerequisites. Settle a mutating Executor before
replacement. Independent cold validation uses a fresh Evaluator.

Admitted formal snapshots keep the [v1 contract](formal-agent-orchestration-v1.md)
and genuine receipts. Ordinary execution cannot reinterpret them.

Use [directory agent guidance](directory-agent-guidance.md) directly in
goal-centered work. Planning reads applicable root, ancestor and directory
guides, the adoption declaration and original canonical sources for affected
paths. Member delivery maintains changed canonical behavior and affected guide
links and local instructions before final prose passes and technical review.
The existing work assessment covers these obligations without invoking Task
phase skills. Preserve the shared rules for coverage, feature moves and missing
guides.

## Goal checkpoints

| Work or Coordinator evidence record | Independent Evaluator | Coordinator progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `complete-work` | `evaluator-work` | `decide-work-assessment` then `record-evidence` |
| `record-evidence` | `evaluator-goal-evidence` | `decide-goal-evidence-assessment` then `confirm-completion-assessment` |
| `prepare-goal-assessment` | `evaluator-goal` | Applicability confirmation then `assess-goal` |
| `prepare-stopped-goal-assessment` | `evaluator-stopped-goal` | Applicability confirmation then `assess-stopped-goal` |

The Coordinator keeps all Decisions and simple operational, approval, Low and
closeout records. Executor Actions deliver `aquarium.executor-handoff/v3` candidates; final assembly
records the Coordinator candidate-evidence index;
Evaluator Actions deliver `aquarium.evaluator-assessment/v3` reports with complete
original criterion rows. Record actual Executor delivery and applicable independent Evaluator assessments
with current fences. Keep Decisions, technical operation admission and acceptance
with the Coordinator. Final assembly is bookkeeping: reuse a complete existing final
assessment only when original coverage, current candidate, requirements and checks
remain applicable; otherwise reassess affected obligations independently.

Scope each checkpoint to its work: `evaluator-work` assesses the current candidate
before technical evidence acquisition. It does not require future review work
to have happened. `evaluator-goal-evidence` assesses the fidelity and completeness
of admitted technical evidence before existing completion and finding routing.
A faithfully recorded eligible Low set can reach its finite disposition owner;
it does not establish final achievement. Canonical Goal criteria and applicable
parent constraints remain intact for final assessment.

Phase passage requires current applicability and passing `evaluator-checkpoint-result`
for actual current work and already-due parent constraints. Retain the complete
original global verdict and unmet/unverified rows; later review or Low settlement
can remain negative without blocking its own predecessor. A faithful technical
evidence checkpoint reaches the original completion and finding routers with
provider rows unchanged. Report fidelity alone cannot establish correction.
Final whole-goal passage still requires global Evaluator `pass` and zero mandatory
unmet and unverified rows. Missing original criteria, incomplete
compound criteria, absent reports, stale targets, changed requirements and
missing required candidate evidence prevent passage. Return a disputed or inconsistent
assessment to the same Evaluator with original conflicting evidence. An unchanged
artifact still needs current artifact and check revalidation after rework. Evaluator report fields never replace original provider completion rows.

## Review and effect authority

Technical route binding, operation admission, ordinal continuity, evidence,
provenance, finding validity and operational checks precede
`evaluator-goal-evidence`. Preserve selected review topology, exact targets, provider
lifecycle, finding lineage, review modes and every consumed ordinal. The Evaluator
never counts as a technical reviewer. Incomplete provider work retains its own
recovery and stop paths; those stops may bypass the evidence Evaluator and must
still reach the stopped final assessment.

`evaluator-work` correction and evidence requests use local followup records before
`record-evidence` exists. Require a passing check for the exact current effect,
target, owner and applicable unconsumed grant before returning to work. Later
corrections retain `decide-goal-rework-authority` as the review-mode classification,
then pass `record-goal-rework-basis` and its exact effect-authority check. A
`fix-and-review` answer still grants only its one correction and next confirmation.
Missing evidence uses a separate local authority check and returns to its actual
owner. Reconcile an existing provider operation before any new dispatch.
A negative verdict creates no correction, provider call, ordinal, installation,
commit or route-change authority. Failed or inconclusive effect authority stays
blocked. For a different earlier owner, use its explicitly authorized manual
rework target and preserve the triggering report, source attempt, criteria,
current target and remaining grant. Never route forward around a required gate.

## Final assessment and acceptance

Final assembly carries the complete original Goal and applicable Epic criteria,
all original work, technical-review and Evaluator reports, checks and exact target.
Include branch-specific Low, hardening, closeout and stopped records when present.
The handoff supplies all original bytes beyond the bounded graph selectors.
Assembly performs no new implementation, broad audit or provider review.
Finite local composition preserves the reviewed basis, permitted verified delta,
exact after-target, current criterion evidence and coverage limitation
without consuming another provider ordinal.

Final Epic assessment includes integration seams and overall purpose. Every
member Task passing does not establish the Epic when an applicable seam remains
unmet or unverified. Final `achieved` requires the exact current Evaluator achieved
outcome, applicable pass and zero mandatory gaps. Stopped paths confirm their
explicit boundary and expose no achievable outcome. Negative outcomes authorize
neither success status nor reopening stopped work.

Integration checks affected implementation and tests against original canonical
documents and applicable guides across member Tasks and feature boundaries.
Resolve stale links or conflicting local instructions at their exact owner
before the final candidate enters review; unchanged guides need no artificial
edit. Any correction follows the existing affected-evidence and settlement rules.

When stopped core evidence needs repair, the Coordinator records the gaps at
`record-stopped-core-gaps` and supplies that record and the Decision to the
stopped final Coordinator assembly and Evaluator assignment. The Coordinator reconciles
available evidence and records remaining gaps without resuming implementation
or provider review. Require a fresh stopped Evaluator assessment and applicability
check. Ordinary work can resume only after actual renewed user direction.

Member and correction acceptance remains delegated under the current Epic
envelope. Record the exact target and authority without attributing that
acceptance to Master. Preserve each normal member, verified correction-group and
conditional closeout grant separately; no consumed normal grant substitutes for
a correction grant. Internal validation acceptance does not establish final
user acceptance.

Only `goal-kind=epic-closeout` may use `validated-closeout` after exact successful
internal Validation. Its separate applicability check binds the unchanged
validated implementation, original Epic criteria, applicable Evaluator evidence and
current focused closeout checks. Changed implementation invalidates the
substitute. Member and pre-validation remediation Goals cannot use it. The final
Goal Evaluator remains required even when technical review uses that substitute.

Prepare the complete Epic result and exact closeout candidate, then obtain
Master's actual overall verification and acceptance once. Leave acceptance
unset while waiting. Record `acceptance-source=user` and the exact accepted
target before final closeout. Delegated member, correction or internal-validation
acceptance cannot satisfy this gate. The carried conditional closeout grant
permits its exact commit after actual acceptance without another commit question.
Staging, commits, publication, installation and rollout retain their own authority.

## Verification limits

Structural and synthetic tests do not prove actual installed functionality or
upgrade success. Follow the target repository's policy for installed checks,
record unknown outcomes truthfully and preserve Master's separate overall
acceptance. Ordinary work needs
no prior qualification, private history or internal model telemetry.

After admitted review evidence, apply the [local-settlement contract](finding-disposition.md#decide-whether-a-correction-needs-another-review) before provider-rework authority. Preserve source negative rows, verify the exact correction and current criteria, then supply the complete composition to the required final Evaluator. `confirm-local-corrections-settled` checks pending repairs before ordinary Low completion. Native backend failures and missing reviewer coverage retain their existing gates.

For unexpected missing topology in a running session, use the shared [consented atomic supersede](adaptive-execution.md#consented-atomic-supersede) after reconciling pending effects and retaining the qualified successor. Preserve history, original findings, review ordinals and remaining grants. Declared rework, same-operation result retrieval and an approved normal successor remain under their existing authority; a missing report alone never warrants replacement.
